SONGVALE optional Windows YouTube access component

Unmodified upstream binaries from Flowseal/zapret-discord-youtube 1.10.3.
Archive: https://github.com/Flowseal/zapret-discord-youtube/releases/tag/1.10.3
SHA-256: 244314ae1c24538a0d751601da8e0c925c843371eec4456eb15f14c4fd6b7058
Individual binary/data hashes are in desktop/network_runtime.py in Songvale's
repository: https://github.com/Loro66/AWUN

The ALT13, ALT11 and general YouTube profiles are based on the corresponding
official Flowseal 1.10.3 configurations. Songvale uses their YouTube-specific
strategies, a restricted YouTube host list and TCP/UDP 443; general IP ranges,
Discord, games, service.bat and other upstream batch scripts are not included.
