AWUN 1.8.0 local desktop, Russian interface and Google Play preparation
Release date: 2026-07-23

The Windows application now bundles FastAPI and the complete frontend, starts
on an ephemeral loopback port and no longer depends on Render. The desktop
launcher, static interface, search states, errors, player, library import,
track stories, settings and My Wave now have complete Russian localization.
English remains available from the language switch.

The Android app is now prepared for Google Play as com.loro66.awun. It targets
API 36, builds a signed Android App Bundle from a manual workflow, has Russian
and English store metadata, original store artwork, privacy/support pages and
prepared Data safety/App content answers. The Play client has no storage
permission or download listener, strips all download URLs and pauses media when
the app leaves the foreground.
