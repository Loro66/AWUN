SONGVALE 2.6.0
Release date: 2026-10-08

Windows:
- Mini player above other windows, preserving the current track and position.
- Tray menu, optional close-to-tray, and global Ctrl+Alt shortcuts.
- One-click update in Settings with official release and SHA-256 verification.
  The library and position are saved before installation; the app relaunches.

All platforms:
- Smart playlists: Recently added, Most played, and Rediscover.
  They update from your library and qualified listening on this device.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+Space (play/pause), Left/Right (tracks), Up/Down (volume),
M (mini player), S (show window). Conflicts and toggles are shown in Settings.

Windows downloads:
- SONGVALE-Setup-x64.exe: per-user installer.
- SONGVALE.exe: portable app.

Install this version once to enable one-click updates for future releases.
